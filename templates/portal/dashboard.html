How to read these counts

Accepted current records include accepted instruments that have not been superseded or completed release, including those already matured. Release authorization alone keeps them monitored. Approaching maturity, matures today, and matured are separate subsets of that total; do not add all dashboard cards together.

Pending review contains submitted records. Drafts and records awaiting correction do not enter maturity action lists until accepted. No emails or notifications are sent by this dashboard.
